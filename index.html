How to read the UV Index

0–2: Low · 3–5: Moderate · 6–7: High · 8–10: Very high · 11+: Extreme

UV can still be significant on cloudy days. When UV is 3 or higher, use shade, protective clothing, sunglasses, and broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen.
