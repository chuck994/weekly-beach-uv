Understanding peak UV

UV Index describes the strength of sunburn-producing ultraviolet radiation at the surface. A higher number means a greater potential for skin damage.

0–2 Low 3–5 Moderate 6–7 High 8–10 Very high 11+ Extreme
Peak UV hours are the periods of strongest UV exposure, not safe tanning windows. There is no risk-free UV tan; UV exposure can damage skin even without a burn. For a tan without UV exposure, use sunless tanning products.

When UV is 3 or higher, use shade, protective clothing, sunglasses, and broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen. Clouds do not eliminate UV.

Sources and data quality

Checking data completeness…

Cloud cover is not a direct measurement of UV. The hourly UV forecast is used for UV charts and peak times; the daily maximum is used to rank days. Missing values are not treated as zero.
